# Research workspace

The research area separates captured context from active projects.

## Structure

- [`knowledge-base/`](knowledge-base/README.md): organized Granola notes, lecture images, the Learning VLA tracker, safety notes, hackathon experience, and startup material.
- [`projects/`](projects/README.md): one folder per research direction, with a question, evidence, milestones, and decision log.
- [`methods/`](methods/README.md): reusable evaluation and literature-review methods.
- [`inbox/`](inbox/README.md): temporary landing area for new pictures, notes, papers, and links.

## Workflow

1. Put new context in `inbox/` with a date and short description.
2. Sort it into the knowledge base and record its source.
3. Link useful evidence from an active project brief.
4. Create an experiment only after the project has a falsifiable question and success metric.
5. Move product opportunities into `product/ideas/` once a user, workflow, or buyer is clear.

Research briefs should distinguish observations, claims, hypotheses, and decisions. Claims that depend on current model or simulator capabilities need dated primary sources.
