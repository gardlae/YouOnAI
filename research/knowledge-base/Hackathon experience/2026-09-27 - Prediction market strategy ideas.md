# Prediction market strategy ideas

Source: [Granola note](https://notes.granola.ai/d/3ad80bec-3df2-4ad3-bd1a-ae68561177fd)  
Date: 2026-09-27

### Product Overview

- Prediction market micro-investment tool: rounds up transactions to fund small prediction market positions
- Removes friction of actively choosing events on platforms like Polymarket or Kalshi
- Positions as “pre-committed” engagement, framing it away from gambling
- Low downside per transaction (cents per event)

### Go-to-Market and Virality

- Primary channel: UGC on TikTok/Reels
  - Prediction market content already widespread on social platforms
  - Plan to seed with organic user content showing the product
- Peer-to-peer distribution modeled on Kalshi’s early referral credit strategy
- Target: first 1M users via word-of-mouth and social virality

### Business Model and Payments

- Two revenue paths (not fully fleshed out):
  1. Revenue share with Polymarket/Kalshi for funneling traffic
  2. Become a market maker directly, competing with existing platforms
- Payment integration via own Stripe link, not requiring platform partnerships
  - Charges a small additional fee (e.g. $0.40) on top of existing transaction fees
  - Similar UX to charity round-ups at checkout

### Questions Raised and Responses

- Market size: not fully researched yet
- CFTC/regulatory path: plan to pursue futures licensing if demand scales toward Bitcoin or financial instruments
- Awareness outside Bay Area/finance circles: acknowledged as a challenge, not fully addressed
- Data monetization flagged as a secondary revenue stream at scale
  - Aggregated transaction behavior data could be sold to major labs
