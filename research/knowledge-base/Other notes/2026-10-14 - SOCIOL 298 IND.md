### Instructors and Course Structure

- Three instructors: Trond Petersen (lead, Berkeley), Ikhlaq Sidhu (lead, IE), Edo Segal (mindset)
  - Alexander Bayen (CITRIS director) involved on the research/credit side
- Course is a pilot, jointly run between UC Berkeley and IE Madrid
- Diverse cohort by design: mix of disciplines, backgrounds, and AI experience levels
- Buddy system: paired with someone from a different background

### Instructor Introductions

- Trond Petersen: sociologist, 38 years at Berkeley, Associate Executive Dean of Letters and Sciences
  - Framed AI as the biggest technological shift he’s observed in 50 years, surpassing internet and smartphones
  - Key dates: switchboard operators (1976), mainframe (1978), PC (1983), internet (1990s), smartphones (2005), Claude AI (January 2026)
- Ikhlaq Sidhu: electrical engineering PhD, founded Sutardja Center, Fung Institute, DataX at Berkeley; now Dean of Science and Technology at IE
- Edo Segal: entrepreneur, art school background, 5 companies sold, 40+ patents; author of “You on AI”
  - Course name: You on AI + X (from DataX), combining Segal’s book with Sidhu’s applied framework

### Edo’s Platform: Cathedral of Minds

- Subscription platform built around the “You on AI” book
  - ~1,000 books in the library (6 years of reading at 4 hrs/day)
  - Includes: Field Guide (AI encyclopedia), cross-era conversations, The Pulse (live AI podcast), Atlas visualizations
  - Book was written over 8 months in collaboration with an agentic AI system (“Sapiometer”)
- Live feedback dial demo: built overnight (10 PM to morning), students interact via QR code on phone
  - Demonstrates speed of AI-era development: previously would require a funded startup team
- Each student assigned a specific book from the library based on their submitted background

### Core Mindset: Intelligence as a Force of Nature

- Intelligence framed as a natural force (like gravity), not uniquely human
- Key shift: from doing the work to guiding and directing intelligence for impact
  - From making things to making the factory that makes things
- AGI/superintelligence as a “smarter person” available 24/7: requires developing humility as a new muscle
  - Orchestration (film producer, Apollo project model) becomes the key skill
- Anti-AI resistance is real but futile: “you can’t stop the Amazon”
  - Goal: build your own “dam” in the river, a stable place to create

### The “X”: Finding Your Project

- Central question: what is worth amplifying with this new superpower?
  - AI amplifies whatever you put in; vague intent produces confusion at scale
  - Craft needed: clear purpose, human judgment, checkpoints
- Project-based learning is core: 8 weeks to build something meaningful
  - Segal’s framing: 7 years of PhD-level output now potentially achievable in 8 weeks on a real problem
- PhD students acknowledged: institutions lag behind, but individual capacity is exponentially larger
- AI usage policy: use AI for everything, but take full responsibility for outputs
  - Must be able to sign your name to it and provide a disclosure log if asked
