# Robotics investment opportunities

Source: [Granola note](https://notes.granola.ai/d/b7839cb7-2a17-4635-b47b-4dde8cc22488)  
Date: 2026-10-08

### Recycling and Waste Automation

- **Nano Robotics**: AI precision robotics for extracting materials from mixed waste streams
  - 95%+ pick success rate; 1,000+ picks per dollar vs. 70 for humans
  - Hybrid model: robot hardware sales (75% gross margin) + monthly/yearly AI software fee
  - 200+ enterprise customers; two IOIs each representing potential $1B contracts
  - 5 signed contracts with leading waste management companies; entering contract phase for NYC facility
  - Raising $2.5M to $5M seed

### Physical AI and Dexterous Manipulation

- **Delos Robotics** (Paul, serial entrepreneur, prev. $50M exit): workforce management system pairing humans and robots

  - 8 deployment sites; first US customer signed in LA; in talks for $40M follow-on
  - 5x revenue growth YoY; targeting 5x again next year; fundraising to scale

- **Creation Technologies** (David): robotic hand designed to minimize the embodiment gap with human hands

  - Modular QDD actuator architecture (110 actuators); focus on durability in harsh environments
  - Key differentiator over competitors: robustness, not just dexterity

- **Aeon Labs** (Lillian, CEO): foundation model transferring human physical intelligence to robots

  - Generates egocentric 2D video + contact/force/3D trajectory from a single text prompt
  - Demonstrated 0-shot task completion without teleoperation data
  - Skeptical of teleoperation data scaling ($120/hr in the US); team of 6 from Meta, Disney, Google, Nvidia, Agility
  - Raising $15M seed; live demo available

- **Dead Reckoning** (Par Nair): touch-enabled robots for aerospace and defense sub-assemblies

  - Proprietary piezo-resistive tactile sensor (~0.5mm thick); ~3x improvement on first-pass pick of deformable objects
  - 4 weeks old; $150K raised; 2 pilots underway; not actively fundraising, seeking factory partners

- **Chestnut Robotics**: data collection exoskeleton for fine dexterous manipulation training

  - Tactile sensors on fingertip and palm; co-designed with matching robot hand (no retargeting needed)
  - Founder: ex-Tesla Optimus lead engineer; co-founder from Waymo
  - Launched at IROS; 10,000+ units of interest; raising seed, targeting 10K then 100K units/year

- **Cortex AI / Leo**: EMG wristband + egocentric vision for robot training data

  - Captures muscle signals, 3D hand pose, and finger force without a glove
  - Glove-level data quality at 1/10th the price; exclusive data partners across Vietnam, Thailand, Europe, US, Japan
  - Up to 100,000 hours/month per project

- **RRA RO** (N. Chapodia): data infrastructure and eval platform for robotics companies

  - Co-founder background: $25M B2B exit; other co-founder deployed humanoids at Murphy Robotics
  - Sub-10-second sync accuracy; annotates data across multiple hardware sources
  - Differentiator: full-stack (data + eval), long-term partner model, software layer that improves third-party data

### Service Robotics

- **Autonomous Food Kiosk** (unnamed): fresh food kiosk in vending machine footprint, 24/7, zero staff

  - New gen: 180 bowls/hr in 50 sq ft (equiv. to 1,500 sq ft kitchen with 5 staff)
  - Unit cost: ~$65K at single-unit scale; revenue share 5–15% + lease/sale model
  - Pricing set by partners ($8–$15/bowl); few thousand bowls served across pilots
  - Focus: retail chains and food service contractors

- **Prasha / Restroom Cleaning Robot**: autonomous restroom cleaning robot

  - 20 deployed; 100 on order; 3,000+ LOIs over 3 years; 81% intervention-free and improving
  - Deployed in Hong Kong, Singapore, US, Abu Dhabi; designed for tight spaces
  - Unit cost: $40K; ~30% gross margin now, targeting 60% in 15 months
  - Raising Series A+ of $5M to scale US and Europe
