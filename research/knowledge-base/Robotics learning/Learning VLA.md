This page is the study hub for vision-language-action models and the supporting reinforcement-learning material.

## Contents

- **Research papers:** 76 course readings, plus 11 earlier suggestions. ACT is the starting paper. The course list contains 30 discussion readings and 46 additional readings.
- **CS285 Fall 2023:** 99 deep-reinforcement-learning videos in the linked playlist.
- **Course context:** discussion topics cover simulators, sim-to-real and real-world RL, web pretraining, behavior models, model-based control, coding agents, world/action models, dexterous hands, tactile sensing, imitation learning, action representations, data collection, memory, reasoning, and evaluation.
