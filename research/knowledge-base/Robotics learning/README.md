# Robotics learning

This folder is the main knowledge base for the planned robotics-learning research project. It combines lecture notes, lecture images, the Learning VLA reading tracker, and a workspace for developing the research question.

## Key materials

- [Learning VLA](Learning%20VLA.md): local copy of the Notion study hub.
- [Research-paper reading list](Research%20papers/reading-list.md): 88 tracked entries, including the 76-paper course list, ACT as the starting paper, and 11 earlier suggestions.
- [Lecture images](Lecture%20images/README.md): 118 robotics and embodied-AI images grouped by date and topic.
- [Research project workspace](Research%20project/README.md): starting synthesis and the questions that can become a research proposal.
